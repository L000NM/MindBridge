MindBridge Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

MindBridge ("the app") is an Android app for loneliness and mental well-being check-ins. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it is handled.

Information we use

  • Google sign-in (optional): if you choose "Continue with Google", the app receives your Google account name and email address. You can instead use guest mode, which collects no account information.
  • Your entries: questionnaire answers and scores, daily mood entries, and notes you write.

Where it is stored

All of this information is stored only on your device. MindBridge does not have its own server and does not upload your name, email, scores, mood entries or notes anywhere. The app uses an internet connection only to complete Google sign-in.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or send your information to anyone. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools.

Deleting your data

Signing out from the account menu on the Home screen deletes your saved results, mood log and notes from the device. Uninstalling the app also removes all of it.

Not medical advice

The questionnaires are screening tools and do not provide a diagnosis. If you are struggling, please contact a doctor, counsellor or helpline. In India you can call Tele-MANAS at 14416 or emergency services at 112.

Children

MindBridge is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
