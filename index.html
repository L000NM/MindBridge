MindBridge

An Android app on loneliness and mental well-being, built for UN Sustainable Development Goal 3: Good Health and Well-being.

What it does

  • Loneliness check (3-item UCLA Loneliness Scale)
  • Mood check (PHQ-9) and anxiety check (GAD-7)
  • Daily mood log with a 7-day chart and streak
  • Guided breathing and private notes to self
  • Learning guides and helplines (Tele-MANAS 14416, KIRAN 1800-599-0019, emergency 112)

MindBridge offers screening questionnaires and self-help information. It is not a medical diagnosis or a replacement for professional care. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.
